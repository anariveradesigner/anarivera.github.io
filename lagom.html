About us

Hey there!
We're Gio & Ana

At Lagom Web Studio, we believe great websites are built at the intersection of thoughtful design and reliable technology. Founded by a designer-developer team based in Barcelona, we bring together complementary expertise to create digital experiences that are both beautiful and functional.

Gio Mendoza is a software engineer and developer with +10 years of experience building robust, scalable digital products. His structured approach ensures every project is technically sound, performant, and built to last.

Ana Rivera is a UX/UI designer focused on creating intuitive, engaging, and user-centered experiences. She leads the visual and strategic direction of each project, ensuring that every design decision serves both the user and the business.

Together, we help entrepreneurs and businesses establish a strong online presence through websites that inspire trust, communicate value, and support growth.
