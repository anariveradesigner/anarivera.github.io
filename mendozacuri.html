Overview

About the project

The client 🔎

Propiedades Mendoza Curi needed something different: a platform capable of supporting the entire operation of a growing real estate business while delivering a premium experience to buyers and sellers.

The challenge 🎯

Managing big quantities of properties became increasingly inefficient. Publishing listings, updating information, organizing documents, and generating contracts required multiple manual processes that consumed valuable time and slowed down daily operations.

The solution 💡

We built a fully custom platform, tailored to improve the company's workflow. Thanks to an integrated CRM, agents can manage properties, upload images and documentation, generate contracts, and publish listings from a centralized dashboard. Every property automatically generates its own page.
